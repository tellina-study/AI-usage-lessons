### 1.5. Last-mile cost percentage

- **Statista 2024:** доля last-mile в total parcel shipping cost — **с 41% (2018) до 53% (2023)** `[VFY-day-of]` [Statista].
- **Driver / delivery provider costs:** 50-60% из last-mile expenses [Statista breakdown].

### 1.6. Routing / OR

- **UPS ORION:** **~100 миллионов миль/год** экономии, **~10 миллионов галлонов топлива/год**, **$300–400 миллионов savings/год**. Cumulative savings к декабрю 2015 — $320M (per INFORMS) [INFORMS]. Original ORION — снижение 8 миль/driver; dynamic ORION upgrade — еще 2-4 миль/driver [Supply Chain Dive].
- **Pedagogical note.** ORION = **OR (linear programming + heuristics)**, не deep learning, не RL. **Canonical anti-hype point** для лекции.

### 1.7. Black-swan disruptions

- **Houthi Red Sea 2024.** Container shipping через Red Sea упал на **90%** (декабрь 2023 → февраль 2024, US DIA). Daily transit trading volume: 4M метрических тонн → 1,7M (**–57,5%**). Прежде ~15% мировой морской торговли + 30% global container traffic шло через Red Sea. **+30% transit time** Asia-Europe через Cape of Good Hope; **–9% effective global container capacity** [Atlas Institute; J.P. Morgan].
- **Suez Ever Given (март 2021).** Заблокирован 6 дней (23–29 марта 2021); **~12% world trade** проходило через Suez; **$9,6 миллиарда** goods held up (Bloomberg estimate); **$400M/час** delay cost estimates (CNBC); Bloomberg ~$10B/день global economy impact [Wikipedia 2021 Suez; CNBC; University of Gothenburg].
- **Trucker shortage US (ATA 2024):** дефицит **78 000 водителей** во второй половине 2024; **1,2 миллиона новых нужны за десятилетие**; turnover at large carriers — **>90% годовой** [ATA].

## 2. Цитаты CEO/CTO (verifiable, attribution-clean)

### 2.1. Elon Musk — Tesla Robotaxi unveiling (October 2024)

- *(Direct exact quote требует verification source.)* Musk о Robotaxi launch / Cybercab production: «We Robotaxi everywhere by end of 2026.»
  - **Source check needed:** Tesla «We, Robot» event October 10, 2024.
  - **Pedagogical use:** показать gap «vendor promise vs measurable execution». Tesla Robotaxi Austin как реальное состояние май 2026 — **10 машин, 14 ДТП**.

### 2.2. Mary Barra (GM CEO) — Cruise exit December 2024

- **Реальная позиция GM (paraphrase):** «Considerable time and resources would be needed to scale the business, along with an increasingly competitive robotaxi market.»
- **Source:** [CNBC GM Cruise exit Dec 2024](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/12/15/end-of-gm-cruise-driverless-robotaxi.html).
- **Pedagogical use:** показать как **OEM CEO формализует AV-exit** — это не «AI failed», это **«business model не worked»**. Различие critical.

### 2.3. Alex Rodrigues (Embark Trucks CEO, март 2023)

- **Direct quote (cited TechCrunch + FreightWaves):** «The last nine months have been tough for the autonomous trucking industry, and for Embark – the capital markets have turned their backs on pre-revenue companies, just as slipping manufacturer timelines have delayed the prospect of scaled commercial deployment.»
- **Source:** [TechCrunch Embark layoffs](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/03/embark-trucks-lays-off-workers-explores-liquidation-of-self-driving-truck-assets/).
- **Pedagogical use:** **honest first-person admission** что hype-cycle не survived public-markets demand. Канонический «SPAC bust» 2021-2023.

### 2.4. Sundar Pichai (Alphabet CEO) — Waymo 2025

- **Public statement Q3 2025 earnings call:** Pichai called Waymo «a key driver of next-generation Alphabet growth» — investment continues.
- **Source check needed:** Alphabet investor relations transcripts.
- **Pedagogical use:** **сравнение** с GM Cruise — Alphabet patient capital + Waymo измеримая operational scale = **survivor pattern**.

### 2.5. NTSB (US National Transportation Safety Board) — Uber Tempe 2018 report

- **Critical statement (NTSB report):** «The Uber Advanced Technologies Group's deactivation of its automatic emergency braking system increased the risks associated with testing automated vehicles on public roads.»
- **Additional:** «Uber's inadequate safety culture and inadequate safety risk assessment procedures were cited as factors.»
- **Source:** NTSB Highway Accident Report HAR-19/03 (Tempe 2018).
- **Pedagogical use:** **regulator's voice** — официальная классификация failure modes. Это **defensible authority**, не блогерский анализ.

### 2.6. Aurora press (Chris Urmson, CEO) — Mai 2025 commercial launch

- **Press release (May 2025):** «Today marks the dawn of a new era in transportation as Aurora becomes the first company to operate a commercial self-driving service with heavy-duty trucks on public roads in the United States.»
- **Source:** [Aurora press May 2025](https://ir.aurora.tech/news-events/press-releases/detail/119/aurora-begins-commercial-driverless-trucking-in-texas-ushering-in-a-new-era-of-freight).
- **Pedagogical use:** **survivor moment** — после массовой консолидации (Argo, Embark, TuSimple), Aurora — первый commercial L4 в US trucking. Это **balance** для failure-bucket — не вся отрасль провалилась.

### 2.7. Bob Costello (ATA chief economist) — driver shortage 2024

- **ATA report:** Shortage eased в 2024 «for all the wrong reasons» — economic downturn reduced freight demand, не solved structural shortage.
- **Source:** [Trucking Dive — ATA MCE 2024](https://www.truckingdive.com/news/ata-mce-2024-driver-shortage-chief-economist-bob-costello/729792/).
- **Pedagogical use:** **structural labor problem не решается AV** на горизонте 2030. Math 78K дефицит vs ~10 Aurora driverless.

### 2.8. Stefan Seltz-Axmacher (Starsky founder, март 2020)

- **Medium essay «The end of Starsky Robotics»:** «Supervised machine learning doesn't live up to the hype.» / «Sim-to-real has very real limits.»
- **Pedagogical use:** **first-wave casualty post-mortem** — honest first-person о technical reasons (sim-to-real gap, edge case slow progress).
