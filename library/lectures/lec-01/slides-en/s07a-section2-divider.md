## Speaker notes

We've worked out what AI is and where it came from. Next — a short section about the present: where AI stands right now, in 2026.

First — the scale in numbers: how many people and companies already use AI daily, and why this coexists with the mass failure of pilot projects. Then — a survey of the 2023–2026 breakthroughs from teams that were not the obvious market favorites: this opens up space for what you yourselves will analyze later in the course.

The section is short — six minutes — but it's precisely the one that sets the scale: AI is everywhere and almost nowhere fully finished at the same time. That tension is the central theme of the whole course.
