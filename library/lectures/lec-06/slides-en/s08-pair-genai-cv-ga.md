# Generative AI/LLM, CV, evolutionary — boundary and alternative

## Assertion

Generative AI/LLM, computer vision, and evolutionary/GA — three classes, each with one real example and an explicit boundary of applicability.

## Speaker notes

Let's finish the overview of the six classes with the remaining three. Generative AI for geometry and the LLM assistant fit for an early concept sketch, a mood board, shape variety for a design review, and — for the LLM — a draft of technical text subject to mandatory review. Not applicable as a source of finished geometry for calculation or production without full manual rework, and not applicable as a source of truth on grades, standard numbers, tolerances, numbers. The classical alternative — parametric CAD with explicit constraints plus deterministic topology optimization plus FE validation; for facts — the normative base and a reference catalog.

Computer vision fits as a first filter in high-volume routine sorting: flagging suspicious zones for an operator. Under controlled conditions, modern convolutional networks show high classification accuracy for defects. Not applicable as the sole arbiter of fitness in safety-critical non-destructive testing: models transfer poorly between rigs and materials. The alternative — normative non-destructive testing with a qualified NDT technician and a certified probability-of-detection (POD) methodology.

Evolutionary and genetic algorithms fit for conflicting or unusual requirements. The canonical example — the evolved antenna of NASA's ST5 mission: a genetic algorithm found an "organic"-looking antenna shape in a few person-months, and it performed as intended in flight. Not applicable when interpretability and explainability to a regulator are needed: an expert cannot "read" the evolved shape. Out of this review crystallize the three anchor questions we return to in every part: deterministic or probabilistic; what is being optimized and who set the constraints; who bears responsibility.
