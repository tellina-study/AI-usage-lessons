advance and depends on the outcome of computations inside the loop itself."
Text B (human, a real translation): "The for statement is used to iterate
over the items of a sequence — such as a string, tuple, or list — or another
iterable object. The list of expressions is evaluated once and must yield an
iterable object; the resulting set of statements is then executed once for
each item, in the order returned by the iterator."
