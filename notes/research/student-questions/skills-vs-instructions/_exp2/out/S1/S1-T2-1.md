# Delete Confirmation Modal Design

## Modal Structure

The delete confirmation modal is a centered overlay window that interrupts the user workflow to explicitly confirm a destructive action. It sits above a semi-transparent backdrop (opacity 60-70%, color dark gray) that darkens the page and prevents interaction with underlying content until the modal is dismissed.

## Title

The modal header displays a clear, action-oriented title: **"Delete [Item Name]?"** or **"Confirm Deletion"**. The title uses a bold sans-serif font (16-18px weight 600), positioned left-aligned at the top of the modal with 16px padding. An icon (trash bin or warning triangle, 20px) appears 8px to the left of the text, colored in a subtle red or warning tone to signal destructive intent without being aggressive.

## Explanatory Text

Below the title, explanatory text (14px, weight 400, line-height 1.5) provides context about what will happen: *"This action cannot be undone. Once deleted, [item] will be permanently removed from [location/system]."* 

For high-risk deletions (e.g., entire projects), additional details appear: *"Associated resources, comments, or linked items may also be affected."* The text uses neutral gray (not black), sits in 12px top margin from title, and has 20px bottom margin before buttons. The tone is informative, not alarmist—clarity over emotion.

## Button States & Layout

Two buttons appear at the bottom, spaced horizontally with 8px gap:

**1. Secondary Button (Left): "Cancel"**
- Background: Light gray (`#f0f0f0`) or transparent with gray border (2px)
- Text: Dark gray (`#333`), 14px weight 500
- Padding: 10px 24px
- On hover: Background darkens slightly (`#e0e0e0`) or border thickens
- On focus: Blue outline ring (3px, `#0066cc`) for keyboard navigation
- On active/click: Briefly scale down (98%) for tactile feedback

**2. Primary Button (Right): "Delete"**
- Background: Red or red-orange (`#d32f2f` or `#e53935`)
- Text: White, 14px weight 600
- Padding: 10px 24px
- On hover: Background darkens (`#b71c1c`)
- On focus: Same blue outline ring as secondary button
- On active/click: Scale down (98%), background deepens further
- Disabled state (during processing): Opacity reduced (60%), cursor changes to "not-allowed", no hover effect

## Accessibility & Interaction

Both buttons have clear focus indicators for keyboard navigation. Tab cycles through cancel → delete → cancel. Pressing Escape dismisses the modal, canceling the action. The delete button's red color provides sufficient contrast (WCAG AA compliant). Loading state during deletion shows a spinner inside the button with text becoming momentarily disabled. Confirmation message appears after successful deletion.
