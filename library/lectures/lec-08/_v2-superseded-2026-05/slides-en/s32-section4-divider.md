# Section 4. AI is not needed here

## Assertion

Section 4 — AI is not needed here. Negative-choice criteria.

## Visual

Minimalist divider: a large "4" decoratively (300pt outline). Title "AI is not needed here" 44pt deep blue. Frame phrase 18pt: "4 refusal criteria + 3 human-only zones + empirical end-user rejection." At the bottom — a 6-card progress bar; section 4 gold.

## Speaker notes

We finish the main failure budget — Section three — and move into Section four. This is a switch of register from diagnostic to normative. Up to this point we were working through what AI did — added, changed, broke. Now we are working through where an engineer is wise to say "AI is not needed here." The section's three slides cover: four criteria for refusing AI in a creative project — derived from the lessons of Section three; three human-only zones — where AI fundamentally should not replace a human; and empirical end-user rejection — the YouTube AI-thumbnail case as a signal that end users notice and punish an AI shortcut. This is not a "list of prohibitions" — it is a set of criteria for a deliberate choice. The student-engineer must be able to apply these criteria to a specific task, not memorize them as dogma. By the end of Section four you will have a mental toolkit for a negative choice. Section five translates this toolkit into an action checklist.
