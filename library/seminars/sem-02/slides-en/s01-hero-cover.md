# Seminar 2. When AI, which AI, and on what

## Assertion

Seven real work situations. In each one, there's a decision an engineer has to make
before they ever open an editor.

## Visual

Large photograph of a team's live workspace — a task board, a chat window, laptops.
The title sits over the bottom third of the frame.

## Speaker notes

Good afternoon. Today is seminar number two, and the format is simple: we'll work
through seven situations together — the kind that could land on any of your desks at
real jobs. In each one, someone asks "can you automate this for us," and then the
engineer has to decide what to actually build. We're not going to lecture at you and
we're not going to present for half an hour — we're going to talk through concrete
stories out loud, all together, as a room. I'll show you a setup, you'll say what
you'd propose, and then in almost every case a detail will show up that wasn't in
the first version of the story. Being willing to revise your decision when new
information shows up is exactly the skill you need on a real job, where the client
states the problem piece by piece instead of all at once. Let's go.
