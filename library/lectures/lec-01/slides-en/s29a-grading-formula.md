# Final-grade formula: 100 = 10 + 30 + 3×20

## Assertion

Final-grade formula: 100 = 10 (attendance) + 30 (exam) + 3×20 (module checkpoints).

## Visual

Slide title at the top: «Semester grade». Below it, one large centered formula line: «100 points = 10 (attendance) + 30 (exam) + 3×20 (checkpoint 1 / checkpoint 2 / checkpoint 3)» with a gold accent on the final «100». Below the formula, a short one-line legend: «Module checkpoints — at the end of each of the first three modules».

## Speaker notes

The final grade adds up gradually, not just at the exam. One hundred points is ten for attendance, thirty for the final exam, and three times twenty for the three module checkpoints.

The module checkpoints fall at the end of each of the first three modules of the course — that is, the grade is built up over the entire semester, not just at the end. This means that regular work in the seminars and lectures throughout the semester directly affects the final score.
