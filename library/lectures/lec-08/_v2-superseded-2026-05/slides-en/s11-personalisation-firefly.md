Adobe Firefly: 22B+ assets in <2 years, $400M direct revenue 2024-25. Lionsgate × Runway — Hollywood pre/post production.

## Speaker notes

Personalization at scale is a capability that lets every customer receive their own clip, track, or image adapted to their profile. Before generative AI, personalization in the creative industry was limited to template substitution; now — full re-generation per user. The IAB 2026 Video Ad Spend Report: among ad buyers, twenty-one percent are already live with agentic AI for video campaigns, twenty percent testing, twenty-five planning. US digital video ad spend is projected above eighty billion dollars in 2026 — for the first time exceeding sixty percent of all TV/video ad spend. Adobe Firefly. By 2026 — twenty-two billion assets generated in two years; four hundred million dollars in direct revenue in fiscal year 2024-25; threefold QoQ growth in generation in Q4 FY2025. Corporate clients: Deloitte, Tapestry, Paramount+, Pepsi, dentsu, Stagwell — all production-ready. Adobe Firefly Foundry, announced at Adobe MAX 2025 in October — bespoke on-brand models trained on a client's IP corpus. For marketing agencies this is a critical capability: a model guaranteed to generate in a specific brand's style and tonality. Adobe Firefly is not only a model, it is a platform: twelve third-party models are integrated on it. The architectural aspect of the platform layer is covered in detail in Lecture 3. What "production" means in the film industry. Lionsgate's partnership with Runway, announced September 2024. Lionsgate — the studio behind "The Hunger Games," "Saw," "John Wick" — provided Runway its IP corpus to train a custom model. Uses: previsualization, storyboards, post-production backgrounds, VFX. Lionsgate Vice Chair Michael Burns on the November 2024 quarterly earnings call confirmed: the AI pipeline saves millions and millions of dollars on pre- and post-production. Critically: none of Lionsgate's statements say that AI replaced the shoot. Mini-failure: eighty-six percent of buyers use GenAI for video creative, forty percent of all video advertising in 2026 is AI-generated. But the Toys R Us 2024 Sora ad showed that adoption is not equal to success — we will work through it in Section 3.
