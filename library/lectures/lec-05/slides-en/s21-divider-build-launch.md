## Speaker notes

Section three — the loop's third arrow, and this is exactly where AI changes the most: the cost of writing the code itself. But the section doesn't start from that fact — it starts from the classical discipline of release, because that discipline is what defines what "ship it safely" even means — a question that doesn't go away just because writing code got cheaper.

Today — release mechanics you most likely already know from engineering practice, but built into product-level decisions, not just technical ones. Next — what AI zeroed out and where the bottleneck moved, and two failures: one a giant that skipped canary rollout, the other a giant that correctly killed a failed pilot.
