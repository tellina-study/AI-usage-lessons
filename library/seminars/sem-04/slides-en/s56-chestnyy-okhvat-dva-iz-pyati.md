# Honest coverage: two of five

## Assertion

Two of the harness's five slots are closed today; the remaining three plus both mechanical layers (hook, process) — Seminar 5.

## Visual

The map of five slots from section 0 (memory · instruction-rules · skills · subagents · access to the outside (MCP)), static, final — the two slots worked through today (**memory**, **instruction-rules**) are highlighted in the accent color; the remaining three in gray.

On a separate line under the map: **"Seminar 5: skills · subagents · access to the outside (MCP) + hook · process"**.

## Speaker notes

"And here is the honest figure, which is worth naming directly rather than leaving it implied. The harness from Lecture 3 has five slots. Today we worked through two of them in detail: instruction-rules and memory, each across three real decision points. The other three — skills, subagents, access to the outside — did not come up at all today, not in a single line.

And there is something else we never touched today: both of the practical layers that mechanically reinforce the text layer — the hook and the process. This is not forgetfulness and not a last-minute shortage of time. It is a direct answer to the question that has just come up: what do you do when a request in text is not enough, even if it was set up in time, on day 0? The answer is in the next session, Seminar 5. There — the remaining three slots and both mechanical layers at once, because they are exactly what turns a request into a barrier the agent cannot get around, even if it wants to."
