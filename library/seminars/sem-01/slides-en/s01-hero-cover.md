## Speaker notes

Welcome to the course "Industry Applications of Artificial Intelligence Systems." This is the first session — and you're here before you've heard a single lecture in the course. That's deliberate: before we start systematically covering where and how AI is applied in software development, finance, medicine, manufacturing, and other industries, it's useful to honestly record where you stand with AI right now.

Today is about getting acquainted. We'll meet each other and the course format, hear the whole group's stories out loud about where AI genuinely helped and where it let them down, and each of you will honestly record your current experience with AI — no judgment of "right" or "wrong." Some of you have months of daily work with neural networks behind you; others have made a couple of random queries. Either is a normal starting point.

By the end of the course, you'll come back to today's experience and compare what's changed in how you use AI and how much you trust it.
