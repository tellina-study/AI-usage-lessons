## Speaker notes

Today we went through seven situations, and in every one of them you made
a decision — what to build at all, which architecture to choose, where to
process the data. Not once was the right answer obvious from the first
statement of the problem — it always depended on the question you either
asked, or didn't. That skill was the whole point of this session, not
memorizing the specific cases. Further along in the course we'll go
deeper: next lecture — how the model is built inside, what happens at the
architecture level when you send it a request. Today's cases, I hope,
gave you the intuition for why an engineer needs that knowledge, not just
a researcher. Thanks for the lively discussion today, see you at the
lecture.
