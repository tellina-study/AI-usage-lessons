## Narrative bridge

## Speaker notes

The sixth part covers three closing phases: delivery, operations, and documentation. Delivery, CI/CD, turns merged code into a deployed artifact; operations is everything after the rollout: monitoring, incidents, keeping things running; documentation turns code and decisions into human-readable knowledge.

Two of them — delivery and operations — are thin for AI, and there is a common structural reason: their input is a state of the real world — a specific pipeline, a live prod, real telemetry — which is not in the text and which the agent does not generate. So here the human practice leads, and AI consumes the pipelines but does not own them. And documentation is the only bright spot on the whole map, the only phase with a clean plus measured at the system level. But, as we will see, even this plus has a paired cost, and it has its own failures — three for this section.
