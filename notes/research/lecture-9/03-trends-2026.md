## 0. Big picture: где мы в мае 2026

**Defense-tech funding hit record** — 2025 = **$49.1B**, нъ. ~2× 2024 ($27.2B). Anduril 2026 round: $5B → valuation **$61B** (vs $30.5B mid-2025). Это **больше**, чем market cap некоторых traditional defense primes 10–15 лет назад.

**Главные сдвиги.**
1. **From "AI as feature" → "AI as integration layer".** Lattice (Anduril), MSS (Palantir), Helsing Altra — все строят OS-class abstractions, а не point solutions.
2. **From "demo" → "production at scale".** Anduril Arsenal-1 + Helsing Grob acquisition + JSW Defence India — manufacturing scale-up, не R&D.
3. **From cloud → edge** в satellite intel (Φ-sat, AI-eXpress, Lockheed Pony Express 2, EDGX Sterna + Jetson Orin NX).
4. **From "human in the loop" → "human ON the loop"** (supervised autonomy) в drone swarms. ENFORCED limit для civilians; soft в combat zones.
5. **From "Western SOTA" → "China parallel SOTA"** (DeepSeek integration в swarm OS / J-20 dark factory).
6. **From "treaty maybe" → "treaty negotiation"** — UN GGE 2025 rolling text как basis.

Источники: [Crunchbase — defense funding 2025](https://news.crunchbase.com/defense-tech/funding-hits-record-high-2025-snapshot/), [Defense News — best funding year ever 2025](https://www.defensenews.com/industry/2026/01/20/defense-tech-startups-had-their-best-funding-year-ever-in-2025/), [TechCrunch — Anduril $5B $61B valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/anduril-raises-5b-doubles-valuation-to-61b/), [CSIS — Russia drone ecosystem report](https://www.csis.org/analysis/how-russia-building-sovereign-drone-ecosystem-ai-driven-autonomy).

## 9. Trend: LAWS regulation moves from "если" к "когда"

- **2024.** UNGA 161-3-13 (Nov), 166-3-15 (Dec).
- **2025.** UNGA 156-5-8 (Nov). GGE rolling text declared sufficient basis для treaty negotiation.
- **Treaty timeline.** UN SG: treaty by **2026**.
- **120+ states** support treaty negotiation; **3** consistent opposition (Russia, Belarus, North Korea); **major abstainers**: China, India, Iran, Israel, Ukraine.

**Adoption направление:** ↑ толкается NGO + смягчающимися states; ↓ US/UK/Russia/China resist binding restrictions on capability they invest in.

Источники: [Stop Killer Robots GGE 2025](https://www.stopkillerrobots.org/news/september-2025-gge-joint-statement/), [HRW — UN treaty 2026](https://www.hrw.org/news/2024/08/26/killer-robots-new-un-report-urges-treaty-2026), [Arms Control Association — UN LAWS talks](https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2024-12/news/un-moves-expand-autonomous-weapons-discussions).

## 10. Trend: Russia's "Sovereign AI" + sanctions evasion

- **CSIS report April 2026** (Kateryna Bondar) — Russia builds sovereign AI-drone ecosystem; Putin Decree #116 (Feb 26, 2026) — Commission on AI development; presidential-level governance.
- **National AI budget 2025–2027**: ~26.49B RUB.
- **Strategy.** "Apply available AI in combat → refine → scale". CSIS notes даже limited AI capabilities produce effects when combat-tested.
- **Foreign hardware dependency.** NVIDIA Jetson в Geran-2, AMD/Dell servers smuggled through India (Shreya Life Sciences case, $300M+ in shipments 2024).
- **Сильные стороны.** Massive combat data feedback loop (Glaz/Groza/Svod systems aggregating UAS telemetry).
- **Слабые.** Compute dependency, chip access, software talent loss (sanctions emigration), foreign talent flight.

**Adoption направление:** ↑ военная adoption rapid; ↓ commercial AI ecosystem deteriorating due to sanctions + talent loss.

Источники: [CSIS — Russia drone ecosystem](https://www.csis.org/analysis/how-russia-building-sovereign-drone-ecosystem-ai-driven-autonomy), [CSIS — Russia reshapes C2 AI warfare](https://www.csis.org/analysis/how-russia-reshaping-command-and-control-ai-enabled-warfare), [CNAS — Russia AI 2024](https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/files.cnas.org/documents/Russia-AI_2024-final.pdf), [T-invariant — sovereign means military](https://t-invariant.org/2025/03/sovereign-means-military-how-russia-militarized-ai-drone-and-cryptography-industries/), [Tom's Hardware — Indian funneled GPUs](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/indian-firms-secretly-funneled-amd-nvidia-ai-gpus-to-russia-sanctions-reportedly-skirted-on-hundreds-of-millions-of-dollars-of-hardware), [Fortune — chip smuggling](https://fortune.com/2026/05/13/nvidia-chip-smuggling-china-russia-iran-export-controls-supermicro/).
