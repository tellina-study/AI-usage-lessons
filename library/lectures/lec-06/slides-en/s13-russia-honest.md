# Russia: APM FEM is SIMP, not AI

## Assertion

In Russia, APM FEM in KOMPAS-3D is deterministic SIMP, not AI: the same terminological trap, on a domestic tool.

## Visual

Three honest cards side by side in an Ocean rounded box. Card 1, "Real achievements" (icon `award`, Primary mid): "OKB Sukhoi — a load-bearing bracket for the Su-57, about a quarter lighter · a digital model built on a supercomputer · LPBF (laser powder bed fusion) · a prototype for the MS-21-300 was worked on." Card 2, "Substitution of concepts" (icon `alert-triangle`, Gold `#F0AB00`): "APM FEM in KOMPAS-3D excludes a substantial fraction of the model's volume while preserving strength — but it is deterministic SIMP, NOT AI · 'AI design' here is the same trap Western vendors use · the C3D kernel is a strategic asset (only a handful of geometric kernels are commercially available worldwide)." ## Speaker notes

The picture in Russia is honestly uneven, and honesty here is itself a methodological value — not an occasion for optimism or pessimism.

Real achievements do exist. OKB Sukhoi released a load-bearing bracket based on a bionic approach: an aluminum bracket for the Su-57, with a digital model built on a supercomputer; the new part is about a quarter lighter than traditional analogues. A prototype door-hinge bracket for the MS-21-300 was also worked on, in titanium versions — laser powder bed fusion.

But there is also a substitution of concepts. In KOMPAS-3D, topology optimization is available through the APM FEM application, which, by the vendor's claim, excludes a substantial fraction of the model's volume while preserving strength. What matters for the thread of the lecture: this is a deterministic density-based algorithm of the SIMP family — not AI. The "AI design" marketing framing here is exactly the same terminological trap as with Western vendors, just on a domestic tool, familiar from CAD coursework. The C3D geometric kernel is a strategically important asset: essentially only two or three geometric kernels are commercially available worldwide, and having a domestic one is a rarity.

And an honest gap: a mature AI-generative engine at the level of Autodesk Fusion essentially does not exist in Russia as of 2026 — only topology optimization is confirmed, and it is deterministic. There is no point dressing this up; for an engineer it means that the domestic alternative to Western "generative design" is classical topology optimization plus manual reconstruction, which is in itself a perfectly normal tool with clear guarantees.
