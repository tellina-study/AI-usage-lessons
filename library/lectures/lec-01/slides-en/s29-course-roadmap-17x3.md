Semester map: 17 lectures, 3 modules + exam.

## Speaker notes

The course consists of seventeen lectures, split into three content modules, plus a concluding exam module. Today we're at the first session of the first module.

Module one — the theoretical-methodological foundation and the industries most closely tied to everyday engineering and product practice: the introduction, which is happening right now; the architecture of AI from machine learning to transformers; AI system architectures — agents, RAG, API; AI in software development; AI in the financial sector and retail; AI in the creative industries and media. Six lectures, one module checkpoint — at the end of the module.

Module two — the industries where AI works at the intersection of the physical and digital worlds: engineering design and CAD/CAM, the aerospace industry and defense, discrete and process manufacturing, manufacturing automation and digital twins, agriculture. Five lectures, module checkpoint two — at the end of the module.

Module three — infrastructure, scientific, and resource-extraction industries, plus the concluding synthesis: logistics and transport, telecommunications and cybersecurity, scientific research, the oil and gas industry, medicine and pharmaceuticals, and the closing systematization of knowledge and skills — cross-cutting patterns of AI across industries. Six lectures, module checkpoint three — the final one, at the end of the module.

Module four — the exam.
