# The Samsung leak — and models already in your pocket

## Assertion

19 days from allowing ChatGPT to three leaks at Samsung. Apple and Google have
already built small models into the phone

## Visual

Two cards. Left (red accent): "Samsung, 2023" — allowed ChatGPT on
03/11/2023, by 03/30 three leak incidents (twice code, once a meeting
transcript), full ban in May 2023. Right (green accent): "Small models in
your pocket" — Apple Intelligence (~3 billion parameters on-device, iOS
18.1, October 2024, iPhone 15 Pro and newer: text summarization and
editing offline) and Google Gemini Nano (1.8-3.25 billion parameters,
Pixel/Android: summarizing voice-recorder notes in Recorder, Magic
Compose — offline). At the bottom — a takeaway line: "small models are
already in the pocket of half this room."

## Speaker notes

Samsung allowed employees to use ChatGPT on March 11, 2023. By March 30 —
roughly 19 days later — three separate leak incidents had occurred: twice
proprietary semiconductor-manufacturing code, once an internal meeting
transcript. The response — a full ban on generative AI tools on corporate
devices in May 2023. What could have been done instead of a full ban? An
acceptable-use policy before opening access, filters on outbound traffic,
or a local model from the start for sensitive tasks — exactly the choice
we just practiced. Now for the positive examples of small models already
running locally right now, not in a lab. Apple Intelligence — a model of
roughly 3 billion parameters right on the device, available in iOS 18.1
since October 2024, on iPhone 15 Pro and newer: text summarization and
text editing work fully offline, with no cloud connection. Google Gemini
Nano — a model of 1.8-3.25 billion parameters in Pixel and Android
devices: summarizing voice-recorder notes in the Recorder app, the Magic
Compose feature for smart message autocompletion — both also work
offline. Small models are already in the pocket of a large share of the
room right now — most people just haven't thought about it.
