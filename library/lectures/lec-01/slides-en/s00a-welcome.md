## Speaker notes

Hello. This is the course "Industry Applications of Artificial Intelligence Systems" — about how AI is used in real industries: from software development and finance to aerospace, agriculture, and medicine. The course is not about how to train models from scratch, but about how to competently select, deploy, and evaluate ready-made AI solutions in an engineering context. Today is the first lecture, the introduction. We'll start with a short demonstration, and then move on to why this whole course is needed in the first place.
