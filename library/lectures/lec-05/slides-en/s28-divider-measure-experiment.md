## Speaker notes

Section four — the loop's fourth arrow, and exactly the one where AI lowered trust, not cost. This is a long section and, for an engineering audience, a weighty one: measurement is where "looks good does not equal is good" hits the product hardest. You know how to write code and maybe know A/B testing from web practice, but you're seeing the formal discipline of a product experiment for the first time.

Today — what a controlled experiment is from scratch, eight classical traps, what AI offers as the equivalent of unit tests for an agent, where probabilistic output breaks the familiar pass/fail logic, and two failures — one about a metric with no guardrail, the other about a benchmark in the wrong format.
