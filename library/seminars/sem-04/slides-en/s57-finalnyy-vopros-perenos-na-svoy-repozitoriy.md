# Transfer this to your own repository

## Assertion

Transfer today's six decisions to your own repository — and check which of them really is a day-0 baseline and which you deferred for no reason or set up too early, with no signal.

## Visual

At the top, in small muted type, with no table frame: "1.1 → 1.2 → 1.3 → 2.1 → 2.2 → 2.3".

Below, large, dominating the slide — the question, with no option cards and no "When" column:

> "Each of you has a work or study repository of your own and an agent you work with in it. Go through the six decision points we have been through today in your head: the role and the general description, the basic processes, nested files — and flat memory, structured memory, task memory. Do not write anything down and do not answer aloud — this is a question for transferring on your own, to yourself. For each decision point, ask: is this really a day-0 baseline — something that was worth setting up right away, regardless of the specifics of the project? Or did you set it up too early, with no real signal, simply out of habit? Or perhaps the other way around — is there something from the universal hygiene that you are still deferring for no reason at all, even though the cost of setting it up today is small?"

At the bottom — a single line in small type: "Not aloud. Not on paper. To yourself."

## Speaker notes

The format is deliberately light and collects no answers: a pause of 5-7 seconds, no calling for voices from the room, no breakdown of "typical answers" — unlike all six previous open questions of the session, this one has no breakdown, because it does not test knowledge but initiates a transfer onto each person's own context.

The closing line after the pause: "There will be no answer aloud. If at least one decision turned out to be 'set up too early, with no signal' — or, the other way around, 'long overdue and still not there' — that is where to start. This is not a question of resources. It is a question of the day."
