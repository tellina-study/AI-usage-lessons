# Your tasks

## Assertion

An invitation to share a real task from your own work

## Visual

A simple illustration — a raised hand in a meeting room / an open circle
of people around a table. No numbered steps, no form to fill out.

## Speaker notes

Does anyone here have a similar task from work — one where the question
was whether AI was needed at all, and which kind? You don't need a
finished solution, it's enough if you can state it in two or three
sentences, exactly the way a client would bring it to you, with none of
the polish of a textbook example. We'll work through it as a whole room,
the same way we did today's cases: which choice is sharpest here, what
inputs are missing to answer it with confidence, and what happens if
those inputs suddenly show up or change. If you already know how the task
actually turned out in practice, tell us that only after the room has
offered its guesses, not before. Who's ready to share? If no one speaks
up right away, I have a backup story, but I'm genuinely more interested
in working through something from your own experience.
