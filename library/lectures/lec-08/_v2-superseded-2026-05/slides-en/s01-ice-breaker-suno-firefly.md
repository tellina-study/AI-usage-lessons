# In-browser demo: AI generates a track or an image in seconds

## Assertion

AI produces an industrial-quality artifact in seconds with no specialized skills — this is a new baseline capability for the creative industries.

## Visual

On the left — the assertion large (26pt) plus a short "3 years ago: composer + a week + $500-2000 / Today: 30 sec + $0 trial" in an Ocean rounded box. On the right — a large QR code to suno.com/create (primary) with the label "Let's generate a track right now." Below the QR — the fallback option: firefly.adobe.com (image version). At the bottom of the slide — a backup screenshot of the resulting output (Suno track player or Firefly image). Gold highlight on the price "$0 on trial."

## Speaker notes

This lecture opens with a practical demonstration that creative AI in 2026 is no longer futurology but a capability available in under a minute. On suno.com/create, anyone with no musical training formulates a request for a track by theme plus genre plus language and gets a 30-second song track with vocals and instrumental accompaniment — in thirty seconds of generation, at zero cost on a trial account. Three years ago the same artifact would have cost $500-2000 from a studio composer and taken about a week of work. If the room has no audio channel, the demo switches to an image via firefly.adobe.com — there, from a text prompt, a realistic image is generated in five seconds that in 2022 would have required a freelance designer for $50-200 and one to three days, or a stock photo for $25-100 with license restrictions. These two options — the musical and the visual — illustrate one and the same baseline dynamic: an industrial-quality artifact in seconds with no specialized skills. Next we will work through what this capability gave the industry, how it changed the economics and the pipeline, and at the same time — which categories of failures and legal risks it opened up. Here, hold on to an important nuance: "industrial quality" in the sense of "technically acceptable for commodity use" is not the same as "industrial quality" in the sense of "flagship brand campaign"; the difference between these two levels is one of the lecture's key threads.
