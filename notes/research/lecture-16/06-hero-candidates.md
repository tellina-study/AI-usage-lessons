## s01 HERO CANDIDATES (foreshadow keystone)

### Candidate 1 (TOP): Permian Basin night satellite — flaring visible from space ⭐ RECOMMENDED

**Image:** VIIRS Nightfire imagery of Permian Basin showing gas flaring at night, 2020-2024.

**Why ideal для keystone-axis (Variant B data × physics):**
- **Visible illustration** of huge industrial scale нефтегаза (2 593 oil&gas plumes detected 2024).
- **Direct connection** к methane MRV section (Q2 в matrix) — satellite AI работает здесь.
- **Honest framing:** industry is huge AND has measurement problem — sets up critique tone.
- **Visceral для engineering audience** — earth from space + industrial reality together.

**Sources:**
- **NASA Earth Observatory:** [NASA Night-light Imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/human-dimensions/earth-at-night/nasa-night-light-imagery-tracks-us-energy-transition-global-volatility/)
- **NOAA NESDIS VIIRS:** [VIIRS Nightfire detection](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/revealing-the-night-sky-detecting-natural-gas-flares-the-visible-infrared-imaging-radiometer-suite-viirs)

**License:** NASA + NOAA imagery — public domain (US government work). Attribution: «NASA Earth Observatory / VIIRS / NOAA, 2024».

**Acquisition tier:** Tier 1 — direct via NASA Earth Observatory image gallery (no paywall, free download).

**Alt:** S&P Global Market Intelligence article «Satellites show Permian producers underreport flaring» — supplementary visual.

## Anti-pattern reminders

1. **Stylized Ocean-palette card с verbatim headline** = MOCK, FAIL (per `feedback_no_mock_fallbacks`).
2. **Stock photo «hands typing on keyboard» / «AI brain graphic»** = generic, AVOID.
3. **Vendor logo как «hero»** = brochure feel, AVOID.
4. **Render не-existing satellite/installation** = hallucination, FORBIDDEN.

**Required:** Real image из identifiable real source, attribution visible на slide.
