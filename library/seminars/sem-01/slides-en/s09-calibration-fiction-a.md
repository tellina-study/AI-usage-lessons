## Speaker notes

Text A is a real quote from Victor Pelevin's novel "Generation P," 1999. Text B was generated by AI, prompted to approximate a similar philosophical-reflective style on the theme of sleep and reality.

On such short fragments, modern models really can do a decent job imitating a recognizable manner of writing — the rhythm of the sentence, characteristic constructions. That doesn't mean AI "writes like Pelevin" in any broad sense — reproducing style over two or three sentences and writing a whole work with an original idea are tasks of very different difficulty. The lesson of this round: a short stylized fragment is an unreliable test for determining authorship, especially in fiction.
