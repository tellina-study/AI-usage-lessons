# One-off call, RAG, or agent?

## Assertion

The third choice: which architecture to trust with the task — a call, a search, or a loop

## Visual

Large title naming the choice on a neutral background + a background illustration
(designer to choose) + a single comment line with no spoiler.

## Speaker notes

The third question today: what architecture is needed — a one-off model call, a
search over a knowledge base, or a multi-step agent. These three options sound
like three different levels of complexity, and intuitively it feels like the
more impressive the task, the more elaborate an architecture you should build
in from the start. That intuition is worth testing rather than taking on
faith — like any feeling that "a serious task needs a serious tool," it has
both supporting and disproving examples in real practice. Before we move to the
setup, let's briefly cover what RAG is and what an agent is — we'll need both
terms in just a couple of slides, and Lecture 3, where they're covered in
detail from the inside, is still a way off, so for today a general intuition is
enough: what each architecture does and how they differ from each other at a
high level, without the details of how they're built.
