## Speaker notes

We've figured out what AI is and where we stand now. Next comes the largest section of the lecture, twenty-three minutes. Four ways to build AI systems: model, chat, agent, application.

The structure of the section: first a general layered model and a "task × modality" classifier. Then a comparison of the three ways through the control quadrant. Then — a detailed walkthrough of each type with a schema and an example. And at the end — a practical tool: two diagnostic questions plus the quadrant, which you'll apply in the seminar.

The main idea to keep in mind throughout the section: these four types are not alternatives, but layers. Each next one includes the previous and adds new components.
