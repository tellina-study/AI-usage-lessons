## Speaker notes

The final slide of the lecture. On it — thanks to the audience and an announcement of the course's next lecture. The next industry lecture — Lecture nine, AI in the aerospace industry and defense sector. This is a switch from creative industries with the widest public contact surface to an industry with the highest regulatory friction and the strictest safety requirements. The parallels are interesting. Where in creative AI the stakes are legal and reputational debt, in aerospace they are life-critical safety. Where in creative AI a failure leads to brand damage, in aerospace a failure leads to catastrophic loss. Where in creative AI the regulatory contour is only forming — the Mintsifry bill 2027, the EU AI Act creator guidelines — in aerospace the FAA and DoD regulators already have decades of structured certification frameworks. This contrast will give one more dimension for understanding where AI is applicable and where it is structurally harder to integrate. On the sources of this lecture. All the cited research, lawsuits, statistics, and URLs are available via the QR code or in chapter.md in the course repository. This includes the Sora 2 release reel, the Genie 3 DeepMind blog, the ElevenLabs voice library, the NYT complaint on Bloomberg Law, the Andersen docket, the RIAA press release, the Arup CNN coverage, the Korea NPR investigation, the Shumailov Nature paper, the Futurism SI exposé, and dozens of others.
