# Section 1 · the instruction file

## Assertion

Section 1 — the instruction file: three decision points about one and the same file, in the order they come up in the work on the project.

## Visual

A large title "Section 1 · the instruction file" on an Ocean background, with a gold number badge "1" to its left. Below it — three lines, with no minutes and no details of the content:

- Case 1.1 · the agent's role and the repository
- Case 1.2 · the basic processes: gates and the improvement loop
- Case 1.3 · nested files per subfolder

At the bottom — a narrow tag: "One file, three decision points".

At the top — a wide 4-segment gold progress bar of the session's sections (section 1 highlighted); this is the macro level, visually wider and heavier than the 3-segment teal progress bar of the cases inside a section.

## Speaker notes

A one-sentence transition: "The project's instruction file — `CLAUDE.md`, also known as `AGENTS.md`. Over the course of today's project it will be the subject of a decision three times: what not to write into it, what to write into it, and where each piece of knowledge should live. The three decision points come in the order in which they come up in the work."
