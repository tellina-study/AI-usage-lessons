# 3 zones where AI fundamentally should not replace a human

## Assertion

Where human only: investigative journalism (NYT/WaPo guidelines), original creative direction, long coherent narrative composition.

## Speaker notes

A slide about three zones where AI fundamentally should not replace a human. This is not "AI is prohibited" — it is "AI as a tool in a human's hands works, AI as a replacement for a human does not." Zone one — investigative journalism. Major newspapers The New York Times and The Washington Post have explicitly published AI guidelines prohibiting the use of AI for primary reporting. The concrete components of this prohibition. Source verification — the journalist must personally verify that a source exists and says what is quoted. AI hallucination of any level in this role creates a public risk of misinformation. On-record interviews — AI cannot physically conduct an on-record interview, and transcription via AI requires human review. Accountability journalism — fundamentally requires human responsibility, because a published story is the writer's commitment to truthfulness, and this responsibility is not delegable to an algorithm. Zone two — original creative direction. Here the main argument is empirical, not deontological. Toys R Us Cannes 2024 backlash. Coca-Cola Christmas 2024 cool reception. SI fake-authors investigation. All these cases show that when the audience expects human creative direction — a flagship campaign, by-line trust, an original brand voice — an AI corner-cut measurably damages the brand. This is not "AI is morally bad," it is "the audience notices." Zone three — long coherent narrative composition. This is a technical boundary, not a normative one. A coherent 50-minute album, a multi-act screenplay, a full-length novel — these are forms of narrative where coherence-through-length exceeds the current capability of AI models. Character continuity drift across five-to-ten scenes, plot consistency across 90 minutes, voice consistency across a full album — all these require human-driven narrative architecture. AI as a generator of individual elements works; AI as an architect of coherence through length — not yet. What this means practically. In these three zones, deploy AI only as an assistive tool under human supervision, never as a replacement layer. If your product positioning is "AI replaces the investigative journalist" or "AI generates a full coherent feature film" — this is positioning against structural boundaries, and the market will surface it negatively.
