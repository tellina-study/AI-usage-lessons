## Speaker notes

Midterm mechanics differ from the usual "test during class" format. The test is taken independently, at home, on an AI-based testing system — we'll name the platform separately, details are still being finalized.

The seminar time itself, on midterm days — six, twelve, and seventeen — isn't spent taking the test; it's dedicated to reviewing results and the questions that came up for you. If the test showed that some topic remained unclear, that's exactly the seminar where there's time to go over it separately, with the instructor and with the group.

This differs from the classic format, where the checkpoint is a stress test during class with a time limit. Here, the stress moves to independent homework, and the in-person time is used for what it does best — live discussion and questions.
