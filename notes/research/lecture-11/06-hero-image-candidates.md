## s01 — Hook hero candidates

### Candidate 1: Tesla GigaCast aluminum casting (РЕКОМЕНДУЮ)
- **Сюжет.** Огромный single-piece aluminum cast underbody на выходе из Giga Press. Photograph industrial scale — рабочий рядом для масштаба. Single object size ~30-40 кв.м.
- **Why hook works.** Visual jaw-drop ↔ pedagogical content («Tesla тоже отступил от gigacasting в 2024 — даже Musk не победил overengineering»). Foreshadow Tesla 2018 over-automation lesson.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Press release / Tesla newsroom**: проверить tesla.com/blog, automotive press kits.
  2. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons**: search «Giga Press» — есть фото от Tesla / Idra.
  3. **Tier 4 — YouTube thumb**: Tesla factory tours have Giga Press footage.
  4. **Tier 6 — Google Images** с filter «labeled for reuse» / news outlets (Reuters, AP).
- **Attribution:** «Tesla Giga Press underbody casting / Tesla Inc., 2024 (press kit) — CC-BY-NC or fair use editorial».
- **Fallback (если Tesla press недоступен):** Idra Group corporate site (Italian maker Giga Press) — Idra.com has demo photos.
- **Source check:** [Wikipedia Giga Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giga_Press); [CNBC retreats from gigacasting article](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/01/tesla-retreats-from-next-generation-gigacasting-manufacturing-process.html) — has hero image.

### Candidate 2: Foxconn Walker S1 humanoid assembling iPhone
- **Сюжет.** Humanoid robot (UBTech Walker S1) на сборочной линии Foxconn, manipulator движется над компонентом iPhone. Striking image — humanoid вместо worker.
- **Why hook works.** Reveals course question: «когда заменять человека? когда нет?» — directly links к Tesla over-automation lesson.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — UBTech press release** (deal announced January 2025).
  2. **Tier 4 — YouTube thumb** (Walker S1 product demo videos).
  3. **Tier 6 — Google Images** на UBTech press kit.
- **Attribution:** «UBTech Walker S1 humanoid at Foxconn / UBTech Robotics press, 2025».
- **Fallback:** Foxconn corporate communications page; Computex 2025 photo collateral.
- **Sources:**
  - [interestingengineering — UBTech Foxconn humanoid](https://interestingengineering.com/innovation/humanoid-robots-to-assemble-iphones-in-china);
  - [mikekalil — UBTech Walker S1 Foxconn](https://mikekalil.com/blog/foxconn-ubtech-iphone-assembly/);
  - [BGR — humanoid robots iPhones](https://www.bgr.com/business/humanoid-robots-will-help-build-iphones-at-foxconn-factories/).

### Candidate 3: BMW iFactory Regensburg + GenAI4Q HMI overlay
- **Сюжет.** Worker рядом с almost-finished BMW; tablet/HMI с inspection checklist generated by GenAI4Q overlay. «Human + AI together».
- **Why hook works.** Embodies course philosophy — AI augments, не replaces. **«FACTORY OF THE YEAR 2024».**
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — BMW press kit Regensburg GenAI4Q** (press.bmwgroup.com).
  2. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons** BMW Werk Regensburg.
- **Attribution:** «BMW Group Plant Regensburg GenAI4Q / BMW Group press, 2025».
- **Source:** [BMW press — GenAI4Q](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/article/detail/T0449729EN/artificial-intelligence-as-a-quality-booster?language=en).

### Candidate 4: SCADA dashboard with AI anomaly overlay (refinery context)
- **Сюжет.** Control room operator at SCADA screen, multiple monitors, anomaly highlighted in red box. Background — refinery view through window.
- **Why hook works.** Process production context; foreshadow predictive maintenance + Yokogawa-JSR FKDPP.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 6 — Stock Unsplash/Pexels** «control room»; recolor / overlay locally for AI-overlay simulacrum (но это уже **mock**, не real).
  2. **Tier 3 — Yokogawa / Honeywell press releases** for real control room имеют photos.
- **Risk.** Generic control room photo может не передавать AI specifically; trapped в «control room» visual без AI context.
- **Fallback to Candidate 1 or 2.**

### Candidate 5: Aerial / satellite refinery image (BASF Ludwigshafen, Shell Pernis)
- **Сюжет.** Aerial photo большой refinery / chemical complex. BASF Ludwigshafen (10 кв.км complex) — most iconic.
- **Why hook works.** Scale of process industry; foreshadow §3 process production.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons «BASF Ludwigshafen»** — есть aerial photos under Creative Commons.
  2. **Tier 3 — BASF press kit**.
- **Attribution:** «BASF Ludwigshafen aerial view / Wikipedia Commons (CC-BY-SA)».
- **Source check:** [BASF Ludwigshafen Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BASF) — has aerial image of Ludwigshafen complex CC-BY.

### **RECOMMENDED for s01: Candidate 1 (Tesla GigaCast) OR Candidate 2 (Foxconn Walker S1)**
- Candidate 1 — more universal visual punch, ties к failures backbone (Tesla 2018 + 2024 retreat).
- Candidate 2 — more 2026-current, ties к course question. May feel slightly «hype-positive» without proper framing.
- **Decision:** Candidate 1 preferred — visual matches failure-bucket theme; failure case is canonical; image is editorial-fair-use friendly.
