## Speaker notes

We have just set out the conceptual axis and the three model families. Now we enter the first section — AI ADDED. Here we will work through which new capabilities appeared in the creative industry and did not technologically exist before. The section's seven slides cover text-to-video of the 2026 generation, character preservation across generations via cameos and Omni Reference, voice cloning and dubbing into multiple languages at the ElevenLabs level, world models on the example of Genie 3, the Russian context with Kandinsky and Shedevrum, and personalization at scale on the example of Adobe Firefly with Lionsgate. Each capability will have a mini-failure block — a small hint at what this same capability creates. The full unfolding of failures is in Section 3.
