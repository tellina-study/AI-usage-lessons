## Speaker notes

The fifth category — data visualization. Two charts of a similar format — a line showing a metric's growth over the years. Let's vote: which is based on real research, which is generated from fabricated data.

Here again the focus is on the measurable payoff: not just "which chart looks nicer," but "which of the two can you trust, and why." On the surface, two neat line charts are almost indistinguishable — that's deliberate: the indistinguishability itself is the lesson.

After the vote, ask: what could actually tell apart a chart built on real data from a plausibly drawn one — the data source, a methodology note, or nothing at all, if the presentation is equally polished?
