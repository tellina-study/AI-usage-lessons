## Speaker notes

Text A was generated by AI, prompted to explain `while` in documentation style. Text B is an exact quote from the official Russian translation of the Python 3 documentation ("Python 3 Documentation Translations," digitology.tech), section "8.3. The for statement."

What matters here isn't just the question of authorship, but an honest comparison: official documentation is written and maintained by a team, goes through review, and is stable for years. But for standard language constructs like `for` and `while`, the AI version is often just as accurate — and you get it in seconds, without having to search for and read through the whole documentation.

Lesson: for technical documentation, what comes to the fore isn't who wrote it, but the accuracy and speed of getting an answer. On typical, well-documented questions — language syntax, standard constructs — AI is especially strong precisely because that information is described, repeatedly and consistently, in its training data.
