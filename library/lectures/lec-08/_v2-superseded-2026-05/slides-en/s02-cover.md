## Speaker notes

This is the lecture's title slide. The topic is AI in the creative industries and media: film and video, music and sound, images and illustration, text and journalism. The lecture continues the course's industry block, which began with software development and passed through finance and retail, engineering design, medicine and pharmaceuticals. This one is the fifth industry lecture, and it differs from the previous ones in that creative AI produces a public, visible-to-everyone, and legally binding outcome: an AI-executed voice of a celebrity, billion-dollar Sony Music lawsuits, a deepfake CFO walking away with $25 million in one call. That is, the stakes are not only economic but also legal, reputational, and social. After a short pause we move straight to the lecture's central question on the next slide.
