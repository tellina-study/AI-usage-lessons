## Speaker notes

We finish Section 1 — AI ADDED, new capabilities. We move into Section 2 — AI CHANGED. Here we work through how the added capabilities penetrated the existing pipeline and changed its economics. The section's four slides cover the cost collapse — a catastrophic drop in cost-per-asset by hundreds and thousands of times; generation speed — collapses from days to seconds; new professions — prompt engineer, AI director, GenAI process specialist; displacement — graphic designers minus seventeen percent of jobs, Shutterstock photographers single-digit values per month, the Getty plus Shutterstock merger for three point seven billion dollars as defensive consolidation. The section's main mental model is bottom vs middle vs top market: AI washes out bottom-of-market commodity creative, leaves the top-of-market flagship campaign untouched, and creates a new middle segment — commercially safe corporate generation for real money.
