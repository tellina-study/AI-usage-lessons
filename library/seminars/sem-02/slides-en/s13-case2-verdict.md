# Support assistant — debrief

## Assertion

Three real options, each with its own cost

## Visual

Three cards in a row: "Off to the side" (browser extension / second panel) —
cost: watching two places at once, low adoption. "Switch vendors" — cost: data
migration, retraining the team, downtime during the switch. "Rethink the task"
— async drafts instead of live suggestions — cost: simply a different cost —
the operator opens the finished draft wherever convenient, but a separate
process is needed.

## Speaker notes

This case has no single right answer, and that's deliberate. Look at three real
options. The first — a side solution: a browser extension or a second panel
next to the helpdesk interface. The cost — the operator has to physically look
at two places, and under load they simply won't check it. The second — switch
the helpdesk vendor to a system with an open API. The cost — migrating
historical data, retraining the entire support team, the risk of downtime
during the transition, all of that for the sake of one feature. The third —
rethink the task itself: the assistant doesn't insert itself at the moment the
operator replies, it prepares drafts or summaries for the ticket ahead of
time. There's a cost here too, just a different one — the operator opens the
finished draft wherever it's convenient, but a separate process is needed to
prepare those drafts. In practice, "off to the side" gets chosen when rollout
time is critical and the team is small — it's faster to put together an
extension than to wait on the vendor. "Switching vendors" gets chosen when the
contract is ending soon anyway — then the migration cost gets absorbed into an
event that was already planned. And "rethinking the task" gets chosen when the
organization isn't ready to spend budget on integration right now, but can
wait on live suggestions.
