## Speaker notes

The Q&A section. An open field for questions from the audience. If questions don't come — three backup topics I've prepared for discussion. The first — where are the limits of "fair use" in AI training. Thomson Reuters v. Ross — the first US rejection. NYT v. OpenAI SJ — that's in two weeks. Andersen trial — September 2026. We can discuss how these rulings will come together into emerging case law by 2027. The second — Sora 2 vs Lionsgate, where is Hollywood in 2030. The Lionsgate partnership showed an augmentation model — Sora 2's capability showed a generation model. Which of them dominates in four years is an open question with a direct link to career direction for future creative engineers. The third — the Mintsifry (Russian Ministry of Digital Development) bill, what changes for RU engineers. Entry into force is planned for September 1, 2027. TDM exception, labeling of AI content, authorship to the prompt user — what concrete practical changes this will create for Russian AI startups and integrators. This is especially useful for the Bauman MSTU students, who will most likely work in the Russian regulatory context. If none of these topics sparks discussion — I'm also open to unrelated questions on any part of the lecture. Especially if there are questions on specific cases — Arup, ScarJo, RIAA, NYT — I can go deeper on any of the twelve cases we worked through.
