## Visible slide text

**Generation didn't retire shooting any more than the volume retired chroma key: the answer is often still "a camera."**

## Speaker notes

The list is worth keeping in full, and there is one reason for that: all nine ways of getting the image coexist, and none of the previous eight has been retired by the ninth. Before 2022 there were eight stages the moving image was assembled from, and not one of them disappeared with the arrival of the model — three new ones were added to them.

Principal photography is work on set: camera, lighting, makeup, set dressing, performers. The unit of account here is the shooting day and the shot — a continuous piece of footage from the camera turning on to turning off. Post-production splits into several independent crafts, and each one is a separate profession with its own rate. Editing picks and splices shots together. Compositing builds a single frame out of several layers. Rotoscoping cuts an object out of the frame frame by frame and remains the most labor-intensive of the industry's routine operations — and the first one the model entered in earnest, which the next slides will get to. Color grading brings the footage to a unified look. Visual effects add what wasn't on set.

Virtual production is a stage that appeared in 2019 and sits between shooting and effects: the background is displayed on an LED wall and goes straight into the camera, skipping the need to paint it in during post. Animation produces the moving image without shooting at all, and it is an independent industry, not a way to save money on set.

Since 2022, three stages that didn't exist before have been added to this list. Generating a shot from a description: a phrase in, a clip of a few seconds out. Animating a frame: a finished image in, motion out of it. Extending a scene: the model builds the clip onward or backward from the last frame. The input to the generative chain is text, sometimes a starting frame, sometimes up to ten reference frames and up to fifteen references per generation; the output is a clip from four to twenty seconds.

The full list of ways to get the image looks like this. Shooting on set. A location expedition — going out for a background that can't be built, the classical and most expensive form. Chroma key. LED volume. Motion capture and performance capture. Stock video libraries. Archives. 3D rendering, where there is no shoot at all. And since 2022, generation — the ninth way, the only one where the cost of a single attempt has fallen to single-digit cents.

The main thing about this list is its stability. The 2019 LED volume didn't retire chroma key, chroma key didn't retire the location expedition, 3D rendering didn't retire shooting. The virtual-production market, which appeared three years before the generative wave, kept growing by more than thirty percent a year even after it — meaning the new technology didn't displace the previous new technology, even though both solve the outwardly similar problem of "getting a background that isn't there."

The engineering consequence is direct and carries over beyond this modality: choosing a method is a decision made for the task, not for fashion. Each of the nine methods has its own domain where it is cheaper and more reliable than the rest, and generation took the place where the choice used to be "shoot expensively or don't do it at all." The right answer to "what do we shoot this with" remains "a camera" in half the cases, and knowing how to give that answer is part of the profession, not the absence of one.

The two columns here are a deliberate contrast, not a way to save space: on the left, what existed before 2022; on the right, what didn't exist before it. They should be read line by line with one question in mind — which of the old stages does the new one replace. The honest answer: none of them entirely. Generating a shot from a description is closest to shooting and to 3D rendering, animating a frame is closest to animation, extending a scene is closest to compositing; but in none of the three cases does the old stage vanish from production — it gets a neighbor with a different attempt price and a different quality of control.
