# 06 — Hero image candidates для s01 + s39

**Summary.** Кандидаты на hero-иллюстрации s01 (hook) и s39 (closing) согласно `feedback_hero_images.md` — real images через 6-tier acquisition (og:image / Wikipedia / press release / YouTube thumb / Wayback / Google Images). Каждый кандидат: сюжет / источник URL / attribution / acquisition tier / fallback. Используй на Phase 5 designer.

**Правило (CLAUDE.md / feedback_hero_images.md):**
- ≥40% площади.
- Real image (не stylized card).
- 6-tier acquisition mandatory.
- s01 — foreshadow keystone (лестница среды).
- s39 — bridge к lec-14 (телеком / кибербез).

## s01 — Hook hero candidates

### Candidate 1: Waymo Jaguar I-Pace на улице SF / LA (РЕКОМЕНДУЮ)

- **Сюжет.** Реальное фото Waymo Jaguar I-Pace edition с visible LiDAR roof unit, на улице San Francisco или Los Angeles, в дневном свете, с пешеходами на фоне. Идеально — фото с camera-blanket или sensor-visible sticker сбоку.
- **Why hook works.** Waymo — **canonical success case 2026** robotaxi, 500K rides/неделю. Открывает keystone-ось «структурированность среды»: city = chaotic уровень, и Waymo это **выживший** игрок. Visually striking — distinctive sensor-stack.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Waymo press kit** на waymo.com/press; Alphabet investor relations PDFs.
  2. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons** «Waymo» — есть photos под CC-BY-SA.
  3. **Tier 4 — YouTube thumb** Waymo product launches.
  4. **Tier 6 — Google Images** на news outlets (Reuters, AP, TechCrunch).
- **Attribution:** «Waymo Jaguar I-Pace robotaxi / Waymo press kit, 2025».
- **Fallback:** Wikipedia Commons «Waymo One» photo.
- **Source check:** [Waymo blog 2025 review](https://waymo.com/blog/2025/12/2025-year-in-review/) (PDF + image set); [Wikipedia Waymo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waymo).

### Candidate 2: Aurora driverless Class-8 truck на I-45 Dallas-Houston

- **Сюжет.** Реальное фото Aurora truck (PACCAR Peterbilt) с visible sensor-pod на крыше, on highway. Может быть aerial / drone shot вдоль highway, или front-3/4 view.
- **Why hook works.** Aurora — **первая** commercial driverless trucking deployment May 2025; bridges «highway = semi-structured environment level». Strong visual ID: truck без cab driver видимо through windshield.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Aurora press kit** (aurora.tech/news).
  2. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons** «Aurora Innovation».
  3. **Tier 4 — YouTube thumb** Aurora launch event videos.
- **Attribution:** «Aurora Driver Class-8 truck Dallas-Houston / Aurora Innovation press, May 2025».
- **Source check:** [Aurora press May 2025](https://ir.aurora.tech/news-events/press-releases/detail/119/aurora-begins-commercial-driverless-trucking-in-texas-ushering-in-a-new-era-of-freight).

### Candidate 3: Cruise Origin или Bolt EV burning (anti-hook variant)

- **Сюжет.** Photo Cruise robotaxi (Chevy Bolt EV или Origin pod) с visible Cruise branding, **либо** photo на San Francisco street **либо** abandoned in parking lot (post-shutdown 2024). Wikipedia Commons «Cruise robotaxi» — есть photos.
- **Why hook works.** **Inverted opening** — начинаем с failure-case ($10B → 0 GM Cruise) для max impact, потом keystone ось «среда определяет AI».
- **Risk.** Может быть «doom porn» tone сразу с слайда 1. **Mitigation:** caption — «$10B потеряно. Среда матерается. Об этом сегодня».
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons «Cruise robotaxi»** — CC-licensed photos есть.
  2. **Tier 3 — GM press kit** historical.
  3. **Tier 6 — Google Images** news outlets (San Francisco Chronicle, TechCrunch).
- **Attribution:** «Cruise robotaxi (GM), San Francisco / Wikipedia Commons (CC-BY-SA)».

### Candidate 4: Symbotic / Amazon warehouse — Sparrow robot picking

- **Сюжет.** Реальное фото Amazon Sparrow robot arm с suction-cup gripping item, inside fulfillment center. Bright light, conveyor visible.
- **Why hook works.** Opens с **уровня 1 controlled environment** — где AI работает massively. Foreshadow для keystone.
- **Risk.** Мysteriously «AI-positive» opening — может undercut failure-bucket. **Mitigation:** caption — «Самый chiseled пример AI в logistics. Объясним, почему среда работает».
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Amazon press kit** (aboutamazon.com).
  2. **Tier 6 — Google Images** news outlets.

### Candidate 5: Zipline drone delivery в Africa

- **Сюжет.** Real photo Zipline P2 fixed-wing drone in flight, или launching from catapult, Africa landscape background.
- **Why hook works.** Strong emotional pull (medical lifesaving), real photo, **rare AI-positive case в дикой среде**. 22M доз вакцин — striking.
- **Risk.** Может feel «outlier» — не совпадает с 75% лекции (которая про road/warehouse/urban).
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Zipline press kit** (flyzipline.com/news).
  2. **Tier 4 — YouTube thumb** Zipline product videos.
- **Attribution:** «Zipline drone delivery Africa / Zipline press kit, 2025».

### **RECOMMENDED for s01: Candidate 1 (Waymo) с fallback to Candidate 2 (Aurora)**

- **Why Candidate 1.** Waymo — **iconic 2026 robotaxi image** мирового масштаба; immediately recognizable; LiDAR sensor distinctive; opens curious door «as Waymo делает 500K rides/неделю, почему Cruise обанкротилась?» — это **естественный entry в keystone-ось**.
- **Fallback to Candidate 2 (Aurora truck)** if Waymo images licensable issue — Aurora имеет cleaner press-kit license terms.

## s39 — Closing hero candidates (bridge к lec-14)

### Candidate 1: 5G/network operations center с overlay AI (РЕКОМЕНДУЮ)

- **Сюжет.** Real photo telco NOC (Network Operations Center) — multiple monitors, AI overlay (anomaly detection visualization), human operator in foreground. Это **прямой visual bridge к lec-14 (telecom)**.
- **Why bridge works.** Lec-14 = телекоммуникации, сетевая инфраструктура, кибербез. Logistics last слайд = network infrastructure first слайд lec-14 — **environment shifts**, но logic «AI augments human in semi-structured environment» continues.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Ericsson / Nokia / Huawei press kits** — telco vendor NOC photos common.
  2. **Tier 6 — Google Images** stock photos на reuse-allowed.
  3. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons** «Network operations center».
- **Attribution:** «Telecom Network Operations Center / Vendor press kit, 2025».
- **Risk.** Может feel generic — stock photo NOC ubiquitous.

### Candidate 2: Container terminal at night, vessel + crane + ML overlay

- **Сюжет.** Real photo автоматизированного container terminal (Rotterdam Maasvlakte II, Long Beach LBCT) at dawn / dusk. Crane in foreground, ship + containers stacked. Может с overlay digital twin lines для bridge к lec-12 themes.
- **Why bridge works.** Ports = **самая автономная среда** в logistics, **demonstrates что мы прошли** keystone-лестницу до peak. Bridge к lec-14 = «if logistics автоматизация possible через AI + structure, what is the analogue в telecom?»
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Port press kits** (Rotterdam Maasvlakte, Long Beach LBCT, Hamburg HHLA).
  2. **Tier 2 — Wikipedia Commons «Container terminal»** — multiple CC-BY images.
- **Attribution:** «Maasvlakte II automated container terminal / Port of Rotterdam press, 2025».

### Candidate 3: Drone swarm + telecom satellite (foreshadow lec-14)

- **Сюжет.** Image composite — drone delivery swarm in suburban sky + satellite в фоне. Или DARPA/Air Force MUM-T (manned-unmanned teaming) photo.
- **Why bridge works.** Bridges autonomous transport → telecom/network через connectivity (satellites + 5G + V2X).
- **Risk.** Может feel slick / militaristic; **может collide с lec-09 (defense) themes** — students могут feel repetitive.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — Zipline / Wing press kits** for drone swarm.
  2. **Tier 6 — NASA / Air Force public photos** for satellite.

### Candidate 4: Human dispatcher с screens — control room в FedEx / UPS / Maersk

- **Сюжет.** Real photo logistics control room. Human dispatcher с multiple screens (route map, vehicle tracker, exception alerts). Emphasizes **human-in-loop** на peak хаоса уровня (Houthi disruption, Suez Ever Given).
- **Why bridge works.** Closing emotional payoff — **«AI augments human, не replaces»**. Bridge к lec-14 where human SOC analyst remains critical.
- **Acquisition tier ranking:**
  1. **Tier 3 — FedEx / UPS / Maersk press kits**.
  2. **Tier 6 — Google Images** news outlets.

### **RECOMMENDED for s39: Candidate 1 (NOC) с fallback to Candidate 4 (human dispatcher)**

- **Why Candidate 1.** Direct bridge to lec-14; pivot from logistics environment к telecom environment — same logic, different domain. Closes лекцию с **forward-look**, не backward-recap.
- **Backup Candidate 4** — если NOC photo не findable в acceptable quality / license. Human dispatcher reinforces course philosophy «human-in-loop» как payoff.

## Cross-cutting notes для designer

1. **Avoid stylized mock vector-cards с verbatim headline** — это **FAIL** под `feedback_no_mock_fallbacks.md`. Waymo Jaguar / Aurora truck / NOC — must be **real photographs**.

2. **Per-image acquisition log в `iteration-log.md`** — designer documents who где взял каждое hero.

3. **Attribution visible on slide** — small foot/caption «Waymo press kit, 2025 / Port of Rotterdam press, 2025» — under feedback_hero_images.md.

4. **Hero size ≥40% slide area** — both s01 и s39.

5. **Russian-language deck** — но photo attribution и brand names стают на английском (Waymo, Aurora, Apollo Go, Symbotic — official brands).

6. **Fallback per image** must быть documented prior to design start. Если Waymo press image не licensable — switch to Aurora; если Aurora не — switch to Wikipedia Commons «Waymo One» или «Aurora Innovation».

7. **Avoid hero conflict с lec-09 / lec-11 deck.** lec-09 imagery — defense (Maxar satellite, F-35). lec-11 — Tesla GigaCast + BMW factory. lec-13 — robotaxi street view + truck on highway + NOC. **Visually distinct.**
