## Speaker notes

For anyone who wants to dig deeper — three pieces. The main one is the
story of Klarna, 2023-2025: one AI support assistant that touches on
almost every choice from today, from an impressive launch to the CEO's
public admission "we went too far" and a return to hiring people. The
second — the story of New York City's MyCity chatbot, which in 2024 gave
small businesses legally incorrect advice. And a bonus for the mood — the
story of how the car dealership Chevrolet of Watsonville hooked up a
third-party API chatbot with no guardrails, and it "sold" a Tahoe SUV for
one dollar. A good example of what happens when you embed an external
model into production with no protection against user manipulation. If
there's time left today, we'll work through part of this live.
