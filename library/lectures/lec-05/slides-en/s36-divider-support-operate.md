## Speaker notes

Section five — the loop's fifth arrow: what happens once the product is already in production and interacting with real users around the clock. The classical base is especially needed here, because the discipline of operations exists for a reason a vibe-coding engineer rarely lives through: between "the code works on my machine" and "the product reliably works for millions, 24/7" lies a gap, and it's closed by process, not just code quality.

Today — site reliability engineering from scratch, what AI adds as a separate observability layer specifically for the quality and behavior of responses, silent drift — a weighty operational lesson of this section — and three failures: capital lost with no circuit breaker, legal accountability for every bot answer, and a success metric with no guaranteed escalation to a human.
