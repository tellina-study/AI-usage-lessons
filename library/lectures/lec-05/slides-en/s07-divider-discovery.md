## Speaker notes

We start walking the loop arrow by arrow, and the first is Discovery, research. This is the phase where the hypothesis of what to build even comes from. Most strong engineers have informal experience "ask friends whether they like the idea" — but almost no one has encountered the formal discipline that explains why this informal experience systematically lies.

Today in this section — two classical bases: a methodology for finding a business model by going out to customers, and a technique for one correct conversation with one person. Then — what AI adds to research in 2025-2026, where it's limited, and three failures that show what happens when the boundary gets skipped. Hold two questions of the section: where does AI amplify research, and where is a real person with a real past required.
