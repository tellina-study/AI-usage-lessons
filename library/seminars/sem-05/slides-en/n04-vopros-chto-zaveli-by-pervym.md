# What would you set up first in this repository

## Assertion

A choice among five reinforcements made for this repository and its state, not in general — and the same question will be asked a second time today.

## Visual

> Twenty-four lines of instructions, four sections — and not one mechanism that would carry them out.
> In this repository — with its submission form, its tests and its twenty-four lines of instructions — which of the five would you set up first? Keep your answer to yourself: this question will be asked a second time today.

`hook` · `skill` · `MCP` · `subagent` · `process`

## Speaker notes

The question is on the screen, and you already have the words to answer it — all five were just named.

Keep your answer to yourself. I will collect three or four voices and simply write them down, without judging: there is no single right answer here. The choice depends on which signal has already sounded in the project, and signals are what we are talking about today.

And about the second time I mean it literally: this question will be asked once more today, right at the end.

---

**Reference.**

*What the room will name and what to say to each.* **Hook** gets named more often than the rest — by those who have already caught an agent breaking a rule: the signal for it arrived in the previous session, the rule was broken in the last scene. I accept it and write it down. **Skill** is the answer from those whose instruction file has outgrown itself: the signal is real, but it arrives over the course of today's session, not before. **MCP** is the answer from those who need the agent outside the repository; half of that stage, the discipline of least privilege, does not wait for a signal at all. **Subagent** — the same role repeats from session to session; the signal arrives in the next session. **Process** — set up on day 0, independent of the project: the agent's claim that something is "done" is a self-report, and there is nothing to check it with until the order is written down.

*If "all of them at once" comes up.* I answer that this is indeed what people do, and the price of it is five mechanisms, not one of which has been checked; today we set up two and check both.

*If the answer comes out about tools in general.* I bring it back to the tree with one word: "here". The question is about this repository, with its submission form, its tests and its twenty-four lines of instructions.

*If the room stays silent.* I name the first card myself and ask who would start with it. The voices follow from there.

*What I do not do here.* I do not judge the answers, do not name my own, and do not reduce them to one. The answer on all five the room does not get today: today two of them get closed.
