## Assertion

Chart A — AI, seconds. Chart B — Our World in Data, weeks of verification.

## Speaker notes

Chart A was generated by us via an AI tool on plausible but fabricated numbers — specifically for this game. Chart B is real Our World in Data data on the share of the population using the internet, Russia and the world, sourced from the International Telecommunication Union via the World Bank, CC-BY licensed.

An honest comparison: an AI tool solves the data-visualization task in seconds and does it well — unlike the "painting" category, there's no question of authenticity or artistic intent here; visualization is a purely technical task. The only question that matters is where the data came from and how reliable it is. Our World in Data is built by a research team that spends weeks collecting, cross-checking, and re-verifying data sources before publishing a chart.

This is the main lesson of the whole game: the neater the visual presentation, the less it says on its own about the reliability of the data inside it. Form and content are separate questions: AI solves the question of form excellently and quickly, but the question of content — where the data comes from and how real it is — is still a human's responsibility.
