## Speaker notes

We climb to the fourth rung — L4, the level of the supply chain. This is an environment of controlled cargo flows: containers, warehouses, commodity exchanges, contractual obligations. And this is the place where agentic AI leads in production deployment in 2026.

Why here in particular? The reason already mentioned in the keystone — the speed of feedback. A commodity-market trader gets the result of a hedge position in basis points over minutes to hours; a farmer on the first rung gets the result of a fertilizer over a season — five to six months. The speed of feedback determines the speed of model improvement, and it also determines whether AI manages to pay off before the environment changes. On the fourth rung the basic market mechanisms — commodity prices on the exchange, currency rates — change daily, but the nature of the task — hedging, procurement compliance, logistics optimization — is stable over decades. This is an ideal combination for AI: fast feedback on short cycles plus a stable task structure on long ones.

In this section we'll examine four working cases. Cargill CMAX — winner of the BIG AI Excellence Award in 2026. Then the pseudo-flow "how an agent does a hedge" — four steps that make the L4 abstraction concrete. Tract — €18.6M Series A from Icos Capital; a data backbone for competing anchor customers. Olam Mindsprint Procuresprint; Walmart × Cropin; Tesco. Then two failure blocks: the cancellation of USDA Climate-Smart Commodities in April 2025 and Verra phantom credits for AI-MRV. And at the end — the Russian parallel: X5 parity, Magnit hybrid, RSHB vapor.
