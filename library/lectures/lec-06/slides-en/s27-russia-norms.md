# Russia: ESKD, attestation, Federal Law 187, copyright

## Assertion

In Russia, the boundaries of AI geometry are concrete: the ESKD barrier, Rostekhnadzor attestation, Federal Law 187/58 (cloud vs. on-premise), a machine is not a subject of copyright.

## Visual

Four boundary cards in an Ocean rounded box, 2×2. Card 1, "The ESKD barrier" (icon `file-stack`): "a 'grown' organic/lattice geometry fits poorly onto the 2D drafting paradigm of ESKD · issuing inspection-compliant documentation for such a part is non-trivial (requires a move to annotated 3D models)." Card 2, "Attestation and verification" (icon `badge-check`): "the APM Structure3D computation kernel holds a Rostekhnadzor certificate · without certified verification, a structure is not admitted to supervised production — the boundary is legal, not a matter of taste." Card 3, "Federal Law 187 / 58" (icon `server`): "for significant facilities of critical information infrastructure, cloud-based Western CAD is not applicable, data must not leave the protected perimeter · the 2022–2024 licensing lockout of AutoCAD/Fusion — a single point of failure." Card 4, "Authorship and law" (icon `copyright`, gold accent `#F0AB00`): "under Russian law a machine is not a subject of copyright · responsibility is borne by the engineer or the organization, not the tool — this removes the illusion of 'AI designed it — AI is to blame.'" No more than three numbers and four names on the slide.

## Speaker notes

The Russian context adds concrete normative and operational boundaries to the general criteria. The first — the ESKD barrier. The Unified System of Design Documentation requires strictly regulated drawings, specifications, tolerances, and designations. A "grown" organic or lattice geometry fits poorly onto the two-dimensional drafting paradigm of ESKD: issuing inspection-compliant documentation for such a part is non-trivial and requires rethinking inspection practice — a move to annotated 3D models. This is a familiar situation from your own ESKD coursework: a beautiful shape that there is no correct way to dimension and tolerance.

The second — attestation and verification. The APM Structure3D computation kernel holds a Rostekhnadzor certificate; without certified verification, a structure is not admitted to supervised production. An AI-generated or surrogate-computed structure, without certified verification, is formally not admitted — the boundary here is legal and hard.

The third — sanctions-and-licensing risk. The forced blocking of previously working AutoCAD and Fusion for Russian users in 2022–2024 is a concrete example of how a cloud-tethered tool becomes a single point of failure. The normative drivers of import substitution — Federal Law 187 on the security of critical information infrastructure, and the amendments of Federal Law 58; for significant facilities, cloud-based Western CAD is not applicable — data must not leave the protected perimeter. And the fourth boundary — authorship: under Russian law a machine is not a subject of copyright; responsibility for the structure is borne by the engineer or the executing organization, not the tool.
