## Speaker notes

Let's pull section five together: operation is the point where the product loop physically closes. A signal from support tickets and monitoring — silent drift noticed by power users, a complaint that surfaces a regression, an incident with legal consequences — converts into an update of the reference dataset, the prompt, a guardrail, then into re-evaluation through evals, back into production, and, on a deeper signal, back into intent itself: what we're even building, and for whom.

This is the keystone callback: the signal from operation changes intent — the same discover-build-measure-learn-decide loop, closed. AI changed the cost and trust of every arrow asymmetrically, but not the loop itself: observing is now vulnerable to silent drift, interpreting now needs segment-level monitoring, not just aggregates, deciding now carries accountability that can't be delegated to a bot. These steps stay human on every turn of the loop, and that's exactly what we'll return to when we pull the whole lecture together in section six.
