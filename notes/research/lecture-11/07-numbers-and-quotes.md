> **«Yes, excessive automation at Tesla was a mistake. To be precise, my mistake. Humans are underrated.»**

CBS interview same day:
> «We had this crazy complex network of conveyor belts and it was not working, so we got rid of that whole thing.»
> — Elon Musk, CBS interview April 2018.
> Источник: [TechCrunch — Musk humans underrated](https://techcrunch.com/2018/04/13/elon-musk-says-humans-are-underrated-calls-teslas-excessive-automation-a-mistake/); [CNBC — Musk admits humans superior to robots](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/04/13/elon-musk-admits-humans-are-sometimes-superior-to-robots.html).

### 2.2 Stefan Hartung (Bosch CEO) — AI as new printing press 2024-2025

> «The closest comparison in human history is probably the invention of the printing press.»
> (Hartung про AI's transformative impact)

> «The opportunities clearly outweigh the risks — at least provided we don't abandon our own intellect and powers of reasoning when dealing with AI in the future.»
> — Stefan Hartung, Bosch CEO.
> Источник: [Bosch — Why Europe needs AI](https://www.bosch.com/stories/why-europe-needs-ai/).

> «We must make our organization leaner and our processes quicker.»
> — Hartung internal memo, 2024-2025.
> Источник: [ceosofgermany — Bosch Hartung extension](https://ceosofgermany.com/bosch-extends-ceo-stefan-hartungs-term-amid-cost-pressures-and-industry-headwinds/).

**Hook value:** balanced — enthusiastic, но **explicit caution** «don't abandon intellect». Это перекидывает мост к course philosophy.

### 2.3 Young Liu (Foxconn chairman) — AI replacing low-end jobs May 2025

> «AI and robotics will take over assembly lines.» / «After plugging AI tools into Foxconn's workflows, software now performs roughly 80 percent of the work required to configure equipment for a fresh production run.»
> — Young Liu, Foxconn chairman, May 2025.
> Источники: [9to5Mac — Foxconn AI robotics replace humans](https://9to5mac.com/2025/05/21/foxxconn-ai-and-robotics-replace-humans/); [theregister — Foxconn predicts AI destroys low-end jobs](https://www.theregister.com/2025/05/20/foxconn_chair_ai_manufacturing_predictions/).

**Pedagogical use:** показать **owner-statement vs measured-effect gap**. Liu's claim «80% configuration work» — это **vendor self-claim, not third-party metric**. Студент должен научиться различать.
