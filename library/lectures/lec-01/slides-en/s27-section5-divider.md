# Section 5. What to take home

## Assertion

Section 5 — What to take home.

## Speaker notes

We've gone through four content sections. In the first, we settled on definitions of AI and why there are so many of them. In the second, we looked at where we are now — mass adoption and, at the same time, mass failure. In the third, we broke down four ways of implementing AI and got a practical tool — two diagnostic questions plus a quadrant. In the fourth — the limits of AI: where your data goes, where it hallucinates, how it becomes biased, how to read AGI forecasts.

The final, fifth section is about what to do with all of this. Six and a half minutes. First, a short recap of the lecture — the three main takeaways worth carrying home. Then, a map of the whole semester, so that today's lecture is placed in the context of the next sixteen. And in the finale — a teaser for Lecture 2, with a callback to the YOLO demo from the very beginning. After that — Q&A.

The main purpose of this section is reinforcement. In two weeks you're unlikely to remember every number or every name. But three takeaways and one practical tool — those should stay with you.
