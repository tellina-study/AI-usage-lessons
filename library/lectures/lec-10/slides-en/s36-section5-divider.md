## Speaker notes

We climb to the fifth — the final — rung of the ladder. L5 — Consumer and retail. This is the highest rung and the most mature layer of agriculture-AI. And it's the only rung on which there are essentially no failure cases.

In this short section we do four things. First — L5 retail in brief: Walmart Eden ML, Tesco AI demand forecast, X5 «Perekrestok», Magnit F&R Forecasting. But at the same time — an important anti-hype caveat: L5 success doesn't prove agriculture readiness, it's the specifics of retail-AI.

Then — the main consolidation of the lecture: the five anti-AI criteria. AP-one thermodynamics, AP-three accuracy threshold, AP-four generic LLM, AP-six vendor lock-in, AP-seven AI-MRV. Plus three inline criteria: AP-two-a architectural choice, AP-two-b non-AI alternative, AP-five cloud-first off-grid. Eight explicit "when not AI or not this class of AI" criteria — the operational tool of LO-five.

Then — the pre-purchase verification checklist. Ten questions in five blocks. A concrete artifact for applying LO-two. And the career landscape — market-based, without advocacy, in generic form.

And in the finale — a closing callback to Plenty Compton from the very start of the lecture. Plenty closed not because of bad AI — but because of LED thermodynamics. And a bridge to Lecture 11 cyber-physical manufacturing.
