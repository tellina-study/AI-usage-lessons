## Speaker notes

The fourth category — an image. One of the two cards is a real painting, the other shows a stylized example of what AI might generate on a similar prompt.

This category is about style and perception, not measurable productivity: modern image-generation models really can imitate recognizable artistic styles in seconds, but the question isn't "which is faster," it's "what are you actually looking at, and how do you tell the difference."

After the vote, discuss: what visual cues give away the human hand — light, composition, small imperfections? Modern generative models are getting more and more convincing precisely at stylizing to match specific artists.
