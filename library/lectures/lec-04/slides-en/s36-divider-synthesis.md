## Narrative bridge
We have gone through all the phases; now we fold them into a working apparatus: the matrix "phase × leading practice × where the human is required", the triangulation of independent measurements, a compact risk triad, and the checklist "when AI yes, when no".

## Speaker notes

We have gone through the entire lifecycle by phase, and the last section is synthesis, where everything covered turns into a working decision apparatus you can take away and apply. It consists of four tools.

The first is the synthesis matrix: the phase, its leading practice, its characteristic failure mode, and the point where the human is required, with the vendor as the last, secondary, replaceable column. The second is triangulation: three independent measuring bodies that converge on the conclusion "the method matters more than the tool". The third is Böckeler's risk triad, a compact criterion of three axes that answers the question "when AI yes, when no". The fourth is the checklist itself, eight items that apply to a task quickly. And at the very end — what all of this means personally for you as students, and the bridge to the next industry lectures.
