## Visible slide text

**The unit got cheaper by three to four orders of magnitude from any of the three classical baselines. Five boundaries are named by mechanism, not by symptom.**

**Selection, rights, and reproducibility moved to the right — and separately, the risk of attributing someone else's win to the model.**

## Speaker notes

The first of the map's six showings with a column filled in. The rows are set from the very
start of the evening and don't change; the "Image" column closes now, and the other four will be
added one at a time at the end of each following section. The map is worth looking at not as a
section's summary, but as a running tally: by the end of the evening it will show where the model
closes out a task type across every modality, and where it doesn't close it out in a single one.

Generate a first approximation — the cell is filled with a number, and filled on both sides:
three to four cents on the dollar per image against fifty to a hundred fifty for a commission,
and two-and-a-half to four hundred ninety-nine for stock. This is the section's only cell where
both sides of the ledger are counted in the same unit, and that's exactly why the section starts
with it.

Bring to production quality — this is where the work moved to. Thirty to a hundred generations
and around half an hour of review per usable illustration, and two-thirds of the output is still
a miss on the target metric. The numbers are taken from the editorial team's case, that is, from
the section's only case where a team counted its own selection process and published the result.

Restore what already exists — the cell is filled, but outside the section's own cases: upscaling
and archive restoration exist as a mature practice, but there's no case with both sides counted
in this section's sources. This is an honest gap, named as a gap, not the absence of a
technique.

Hold exact measurements — a failure, and a mechanism-level failure: there are no dimensions,
tolerances, or build tree inside the picture, and none will appear in the next model version. A
cell with a failure is just as much a result as a cell with a number, and for an engineering
audience it's even more useful: it answers the question "is it even worth trying" before any
time is spent.

Fit the real-time budget — not shown in this section. The closest measurement nearby: around
three seconds a frame for a distilled model against twenty for an undistilled one on the same
card. This isn't a frame budget, and the two can't be passed off as the same thing: the
difference between "a picture in three seconds" and "a frame in sixteen milliseconds" is a
difference in the class of task, and it will become visible in the section on the 3D scene.

Answer "who is accountable" — empty by construction. Provenance for a generated frame physically
doesn't exist: every training example's contribution is smeared across billions of weights, and
the check gets pushed outward — through reverse image search, signed metadata, a request log.
This isn't a vendor's shortcoming, and it isn't a temporary state of the industry.

Below the grid runs the payment-address band — a number confirming what the section named at the
very start, on the stage-description slide: selection, rights, discernment. This section's
address is double: selection — all three detailed cases, and rights — the legal case, the
training-architecture case, and the mentions. Discernment doesn't get a full treatment in this
section, and that's a property of the modality, named in advance: a still image has no signature
that confirms itself. In the section on voice, this cell fills in first.

And the section's conclusion, worth saying in full. The unit got cheaper by three to four orders
of magnitude from any of the three classical baselines — and specifically "from any of the
three," because the choice of baseline inside the classics alone shifts the gap by two orders of
magnitude. Five boundaries are named by mechanism, not by symptom: text inside the image,
counting objects, consistency between generations, exact measurements, provenance. Selection,
rights, and reproducibility moved to the right. And separately — the risk of attributing someone
else's win to the model: of the section's three detailed cases, one showed this risk directly.
