# Questions

## Speaker notes

A separate slide for questions from the room, not merged with the closing (per the course's pattern — Q&A stands as its own slide, not merged into the previous one).

Backup material for answering questions is gathered in the chapter ("Q&A backup," part 3) and doesn't appear on the slide — it's reference material for the instructor, covering intersections between sections and questions about the lecture's own methodology: for example, why the random forest in AEGIS still counts as AI; why a lecture about AI pays so much attention to what classical methods do on their own, with no training at all; why the NeBula/LAMP architecture is carried as the anchor example at the start of the lecture rather than framed as a fourteenth detailed breakdown; and an exercise applying the criterion "invented under the frontier's pressure, or carried over ready-made" to a new case — an autonomous inspector for underwater pipelines.

If the room stays silent, one question is prepared in advance in the chapter's backup material: about the sign an engineer uses to tell "invented under the frontier's pressure" apart from "carried over ready-made from softer conditions" — the same criterion the lecture applied thirteen times in a row.
