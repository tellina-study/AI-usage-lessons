## Speaker notes

Google DeepMind Genie 3 — public release on January 29, 2026 — represents a different class of tool that is often mistakenly compared with video generators. What Genie 3 does: from a text prompt — for example, "a medieval castle on a mountain, day, light wind" — the model generates a playable 3D world. This is an explorable, navigable environment in real time at twenty-four frames per second, resolution 720p, consistency maintained for several minutes. This is not a sequence of predefined frames as with Sora or Veo or Runway — it is an interactive environment reacting to user actions: camera, movement, object manipulation. Architecturally, Genie 3 is a combination of latent diffusion for visual generation with a world-model part that handles state transitions. Access — for US subscribers to Google AI Ultra. Anti-hype: Genie 3 is not a video generator. It is a simulated-environment generator. Direct production in the creative industries is so far edge cases. A couple of game studios are experimenting with Genie 3 for prototyping levels. A couple of film studios — for location scouting, generating a virtual location instead of traveling to a real one. The education sector — for immersive learning environments. Frontier-grade use in production is ahead, by the estimate of industry analysts. Genie 3 is an example of the symbiosis of several architectures in one AI system: latent diffusion like Sora plus a reinforcement-learning state model like game AI plus a transformer for language understanding. This illustrates the thesis: AI systems of 2026 are composite architectures, not monolithic models.
