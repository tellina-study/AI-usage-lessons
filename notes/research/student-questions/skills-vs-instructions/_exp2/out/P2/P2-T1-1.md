# Product Card Design Specification

## Component Structure

A product card serves as a self-contained, interactive unit displaying essential product information and enabling user purchase decisions. The card employs a vertical layout optimized for grid-based product catalogs.

### Visual Hierarchy

**Title (Header)**
- Positioned at the top of the card
- Font: 16px, semi-bold weight, dark gray (#212529)
- Line height: 1.4 for text wrapping
- Maximum 2–3 lines; overflow truncates with ellipsis
- Communicates the product name clearly and concisely

**Price Section (Middle)**
- Displayed prominently below the title
- Current price: 24px, bold, primary color (#065A82)
- Original price (if on sale): crossed-out, 14px, lighter gray (#6c757d)
- Discount badge (optional): "–20%" label in red (#dc3545) background, 12px

**Action Button (Footer)**
- Full-width button spanning card bottom
- Default label: "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now"
- Height: 44px (mobile-friendly touch target)

## Button States

**Default (Enabled)**
- Background: Ocean blue (#065A82)
- Text: white, 14px, medium weight
- Cursor: pointer
- Visual: subtle box shadow for depth

**Hover**
- Background: darker blue (#054660)
- Transition: 200ms ease
- Cursor: pointer
- Subtle lift effect (shadow increase)

**Active (Pressed)**
- Background: darkest blue (#042e47)
- No shadow (pressed appearance)
- Immediate transition (0ms)

**Disabled**
- Background: light gray (#e9ecef)
- Text: medium gray (#6c757d)
- Cursor: not-allowed
- No hover effects
- Typically used when item is out of stock

**Loading State**
- Background: Ocean blue with reduced opacity
- Spinner animation: centered, 16px
- Text hidden or replaced with "Adding..."
- Button non-clickable during operation

## Additional Features

- Border radius: 8px for card, 4px for button
- Padding: 16px internal spacing
- Card shadow: subtle drop-shadow for elevation (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1))
- Responsive: adapts to mobile (full width) and desktop (grid layout)
