## Speaker notes

We've introduced ourselves and named the course's central question. Now comes the first substantive section: what AI is.

The section's structure is short but dense. First — why there are so many definitions of AI and where the moving-target effect comes from. Then a fact bridge: the idea of the formal neuron is older than the term «artificial intelligence» itself. And at the end of the section — a compressed seventy-year timeline of the discipline with a clear turning point in 2017.

By the end of the section you'll have a common language for talking about AI: where the different definitions came from, why they don't contradict one another, and at what moment in history AI became what it is now.
