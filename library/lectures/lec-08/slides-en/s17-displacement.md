## Speaker notes

In parallel with the appearance of new professions, old ones are being displaced. This is not a debatable hypothesis — it is already a measurable process with concrete figures. Graphic designers on Upwork: minus seventeen point zero one percent of jobs in the graphic-design category after the release of the main GenAI image tools — Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion XL. Wage compression: AI detection in forty percent of copywriter jobs paying ten-to-nineteen dollars per hour, versus under ten percent in jobs at sixty-plus dollars per hour. In other words, wage compression from the bottom: AI tools wash out precisely low-budget freelance, leaving high-value specialized roles untouched. This is structurally important: the classic prediction "AI augments human professionals" works only for the middle and top segment; the bottom segment is displacement, not augmentation. Stock photographers. Shutterstock contributors with hundreds of photo uploads per month have dropped to single-digit values per month, per microstock forums. Getty Creative segment revenue fell minus five percent year-over-year in 2024. Voice actors. The SAG-AFTRA strike of 2023 — July 14 to November 9 — was motivated directly by AI-displacement risks. In parallel, the adoption of ElevenLabs in the corporate sector led to mass displacement of Korean and foreign voice actors for commodity dubbing. Industry consolidation as a response. Getty plus Shutterstock merger — announced January 2025, deal value three point seven billion dollars, expected savings of one hundred fifty-to-two hundred million over three years. This is defensive consolidation against AI disruption. In parallel, Shutterstock licenses content to AI companies — one hundred four million in 2023, one hundred thirty-eight in 2024, two hundred fifty million projected for 2027. This is a pivot: Shutterstock stops earning from selling photos to photographers and moves to selling data to AI companies. For photographers this is structurally negative — they stop being a customer and become a data source. Hollywood: SAG-AFTRA plus WGA AI clauses of 2023 — Digital Replicas, Synthetic Performers. 2026 — a four-year extension. What these clauses do not cover: bottom-segment freelance, B-roll actors, extras, commodity voiceover — this market keeps shrinking.
