# New professions: prompt engineer, AI director, continuity supervisor

## Assertion

New professions: prompt engineer, AI director, AI-process specialist, continuity supervisor. Upwork 70% year-over-year in AI/ML; 52% of gross services volume — AI-related.

## Speaker notes

The cost and speed collapse created new market roles in the creative industries. These roles are not "AI replaced the designer," they sit between the AI tool and the final client deliverable. Prompt engineer and AI artist — a specialist who shapes prompts and post-processing to obtain production-ready output. Exists as a separate category on Fiverr and Upwork; starting rates on Upwork are twenty-five-to-eighty dollars per hour per 2026 data. AI director and AI music producer — supervise model output, bringing it to production-ready through iterations, post-processing, multimodal integration. An analog of the art director, but for the AI pipeline. AI-process specialist — an integrator of AI tools into studios' existing production pipelines. For example, does the Lionsgate plus Adobe Firefly Foundry integration for a marketing agency. Continuity supervisor — a relatively new role: a person who checks character and scene continuity across AI-generated multi-frame sequences. Growth metrics. Upwork internal data 2025-2026: seventy percent year-over-year growth in the AI/ML subcategory. Fifty-two percent gross services volume growth — AI-related. Per Upwork and MBO Partners reports, independent workers with AI/ML skills earn premium rates relative to the general freelance market. What "new professions" means. This is not a million new designers. This is a specialized class of workers between the AI tool and the client deliverable. They are necessary because raw AI output is not equal to production-ready output: curation, post-processing, brand alignment, legal review are needed. This class grows fast, but it is smaller than the displaced class — we will work through this on the next slide.
